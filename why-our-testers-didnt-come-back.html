why our testers loved nuri but didn’t come back

(and what we did about it)

BY NURI NOVEMBER 2026 CHRONICALLY ♡
the app launches
next month ♡
iOS + ANDROID ✦

big news first: the nuri app launches next month!! and before it does, we want to share how we got here, because going mobile wasn’t the original plan.

when we launched our web app (nurirecipes.com), the response blew us away. the reviews were great, testers loved the idea, and over and over we heard some version of “i wish this existed years ago.” for a small team building this from scratch, that meant everything. it told us we were onto something.

BUT THE DATA TOLD US SOMETHING ELSE TOO

most testers explored the web app once and didn’t come back.

that was a hard thing to look at, but it taught us something important. the problem was never that women didn’t want trusted, RD-backed food guidance. it was that nuri didn’t fit into their lives yet.

if you’re managing a chronic condition, you already have enough on your plate (pun intended). you don’t need one more website to remember to open.

the lesson ♡
nuri needed to fit your lifestyle, not be another thing you have to make time for.

so we rebuilt it as an app.

if you’ve tried our web app, here’s what’s different

1. it’s built around ease of use.

we prioritized making nuri as intuitive and easy to use as possible. you can now spend a few minutes on your commute planning your meals for the day or week, and know exactly what to pick up on your way home. it’s all in your pocket.

2. same core, plus symptom logging.

the functions you know are still here: recipes and grocery lists. we’ve also added symptom logging, so you can start spotting patterns in your own eating and get a clearer picture of what actually works for you.

3. every recipe still comes from RDs.

that hasn’t changed and never will. every recipe is created by registered dietitians, with dietitian tips that explain the why behind it.

4. it’s personalized to you.

you build your own profile, so everything you see is tailored to you instead of generic “healthy eating” advice.

5. we built for iOS AND android!!

no one gets left out.

managing a chronic condition will always take some work. our goal is to make the food part of it feel a little lighter, a little less lonely, and a lot less like guesswork.

the app launches next month, so get on the waitlist to be the first to know when it’s live. we can’t wait for you to try it <3
