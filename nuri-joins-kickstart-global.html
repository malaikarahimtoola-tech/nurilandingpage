we’re officially part of kickstart global

A small detour before we get into branding (it’s coming in #4, promise): some news we’ve been sitting on and can finally share.

BY NURI OCTOBER 2026 CHRONICALLY ♡
big news ♡
NURI x KICKSTART ✦

Nuri is officially part of the Kickstart Global fellowship!

Kickstart is the UK’s largest cross-university incubator for, in their words, “relentlessly ambitious outliers”. With 100+ startups built, £33m+ raised and a 0.1% acceptance rate across 3,000+ applications, we’re so grateful to have been selected and can’t wait to get to know the other fellows.

When we started Nuri, it was two friends who had the same conversation over and over: why isn’t there a place for women managing chronic conditions to get food guidance they can actually trust? Since then it’s grown into 20+ conversations with RDs, 50+ conversations with women living with these conditions, 300+ people testing our web app, 8 founding RDs, and now a beta app. But for most of that time, it’s still felt like us figuring it out on our own.

Which, funnily enough, is exactly the feeling we’re building Nuri to solve. So being a part of a community, and being backed by a group of people who’ve done this before, means a lot.

what this means for Nuri

1. Building the app properly.

Ongoing mentorship, technical resources, and exposure to established founders means we can finally set up the infrastructure Nuri needs to grow, not just scrape by day by day.

2. Learning from people further down the road.

We now have access to mentors and founders who’ve been exactly where we are, and who have built, raised, and scaled companies. There are so many questions we’ve been answering through trial and error (and a lot of googling), so having people we can ask directly is going to change how fast we move.

3. Building alongside other startups.

One of the best parts of this so far has been meeting the other fellows, from one founding a sustainable slipper company to others streamlining the process of learning to drive. It’s a room full of people building ambitious things across every industry, and being around that energy makes the hard days feel a lot less lonely. We’re excited to share notes and learn from each other.

P.S. We’re also really grateful to everyone who’s tested the app, sent us feedback, shared our posts, or just listened to us talk about Nuri for the hundredth time. None of this happens without you <3
